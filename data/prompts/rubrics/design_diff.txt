Dimensions for comparative design audit (score each site 0-10 per dimension):
- colors: palette coherence, contrast, distinctiveness of brand colors
- typography: font choices, hierarchy, readability
- layout: structure, grid, whitespace, visual hierarchy
- components: cards, buttons, navigation quality and consistency
- mobile_vs_desktop: responsive quality, mobile navigation, content parity
- brand_feel: overall polish, memorability, how distinct the visual identity is
Focus on DIFFERENCES between the sites; winner is optional — the diff story matters more.
